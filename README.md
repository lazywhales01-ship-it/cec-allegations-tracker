# CEC allegations tracker

Static page with two tabs:

1. **Allegations timeline** – dated record of the "vote chori" allegations against Chief Election Commissioner Gyanesh Kumar and the Election Commission of India's responses (Aug 2025 – Oct 2026), with news sources.
2. **Maharashtra voter-roll check** – Lok Sabha (May 2024) vs Vidhan Sabha (Nov 2024) electors by seat, winners, winning margins, and the seats where voters added exceed the winning margin.

`Maharashtra_voter_roll_check_2024.xlsx` holds the full dataset (288 assembly seats with live formulas, 48 Lok Sabha seats, notes and sources).

Data sources: ECI, CEO Maharashtra, ECI results pages, Wikipedia constituency pages reproducing ECI results, The Wire (RTI figures). See the Notes sheet for details and limitations.
