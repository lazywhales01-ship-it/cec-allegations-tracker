Vote Chori and the Chief Election Commissioner

A dated record of the allegations against Chief Election Commissioner Gyanesh Kumar, who raised them, and how the Election Commission of India (ECI) answered: by letter, by press conference, and by press note. Each entry links to the news reports it is drawn from. Compiled 10 October 2026.

Background

  1. 17–19 Feb 2025Appointment

    Gyanesh Kumar becomes the 26th CEC

    A selection panel chaired by PM Modi chose Kumar, the first CEC appointed under the 2023 Chief Election Commissioner and Other Election Commissioners Act. Rahul Gandhi, a panel member, submitted a dissent note. Haryana Chief Secretary Vivek Joshi joined as Election Commissioner alongside Sukhbir Singh Sandhu.

    ETV BharatThe Tribune
Phase 1

The "vote chori" charge and the affidavit challenge

August to November 2025. The opposition alleges manipulated voter rolls; the ECI and state Chief Electoral Officers (CEOs) demand sworn evidence.

  1. 7 Aug 2025Allegation

    Rahul Gandhi's Mahadevapura presentation

    At a New Delhi press conference, Gandhi alleged that more than one lakh votes were "stolen" in the Mahadevapura segment of Bangalore Central during the 2024 Lok Sabha polls, through duplicate voters, fake or invalid addresses, bulk single-address voters, invalid photos and misuse of Form 6.

    Sunday GuardianDeccan Herald
  2. 7 Aug 2025ECI · Letter

    Karnataka CEO writes: sign a declaration under oath

    Within hours, the Karnataka CEO wrote to Gandhi asking him to sign and return a declaration under Rule 20(3)(b) of the Registration of Electors Rules, 1960, with the names of the electors concerned. The CEOs of Haryana and Maharashtra sent similar letters about his earlier claims in those states. AICC general secretary K.C. Venugopal said the party would respond with evidence.

    The StatesmanKerala Kaumudi
  3. 10 Aug 2025ECI · Notice

    Notice on the "voted twice" claim

    The Karnataka CEO asked Gandhi for the documents behind his claim that voter Shakun Rani voted twice. The notice said she told inquiry officials she voted once, and that the tick-marked document shown at the press conference was not issued by the polling officer. The Haryana CEO gave him 10 days to submit documents and a signed declaration. The ECI said he could still sign the declaration or apologise.

    All India Radio (newsonair)Navhind Times
  4. 17 Aug 2025ECI · Press conference

    CEC Gyanesh Kumar's seven-day ultimatum

    In his first press conference since the allegations, flanked by both Election Commissioners, Kumar said Gandhi must file a signed affidavit within seven days or apologise; otherwise the claims would be treated as baseless. There is no third option. He defended the Bihar Special Intensive Revision (SIR) and did not name Gandhi. Deccan Herald noted he did not answer questions on why no oath was sought from BJP MP Anurag Thakur over similar claims. The same day, Gandhi launched the Voter Adhikar Yatra in Bihar.

    PTI via The WeekDeccan Herald
  5. 18 Aug 2025Allegation

    Gandhi names all three commissioners

    Speaking at Gaya, Bihar, Gandhi directly attacked Kumar and ECs Sandhu and Joshi, and warned of action against them if the INDIA bloc formed the government.

    PTI via The Week
  6. 18 Sep 2025Allegation

    Aland deletions: "CEC is protecting vote chors"

    Gandhi alleged about 6,018 voters in Karnataka's Aland constituency were targeted for deletion using software and out-of-state mobile numbers, and said the Karnataka CID had written to the ECI 18 times in 18 months without getting the data it sought. He accused Kumar personally of shielding those responsible.

    The WireBusiness Today
  7. 18 Sep 2025ECI · Statement

    ECI calls the charge incorrect and baseless

    The ECI replied the same day that members of the public cannot delete any vote online, and that no deletion happens without the affected voter being heard. It acknowledged that an FIR was filed in 2023 over unsuccessful deletion attempts in Aland.

    PTI via The WeekPTI via The Week (2)
  8. 5 Nov 2025Allegation

    The "H Files" on Haryana

    Before Bihar's first phase of polling, Gandhi alleged that 25 lakh fake or duplicate entries, about one in eight voters, were in Haryana's 2024 rolls, including one photo he said appeared on 22 voter IDs.

    India.com
  9. 5 Nov 2025ECI · Rebuttal

    "Zero appeals" against Haryana rolls

    ECI sources and the Haryana CEO said Congress had filed no objections or appeals during roll revision, that draft rolls were shared with parties on 2 Aug 2024, and that 23 election petitions were filed after results, where Gandhi could place his evidence.

    ETV Bharat
Phase 2

The removal motions

March to July 2026. For the first time, MPs seek a CEC's removal through the procedure used for Supreme Court judges.

  1. 12–13 Mar 2026Parliament

    First-ever notice to remove a CEC

    130 Lok Sabha MPs and 63 Rajya Sabha MPs from INDIA bloc parties signed notices listing seven charges, from partisan conduct to obstructing investigations and mass disenfranchisement. The TMC cited deletions during West Bengal's SIR. Union Minister S.P. Singh Baghel said the move showed no faith in constitutional institutions.

    ETV BharatAll India Radio (newsonair)
  2. 6 Apr 2026Parliament

    Speaker and Chairman reject the notices

    Lok Sabha Speaker Om Birla and Rajya Sabha Chairman C.P. Radhakrishnan declined to admit the notices. The Rajya Sabha bulletin said the decision followed the constitutional and statutory provisions on removal.

    The QuintBusiness Standard
  3. 24 Apr 2026Parliament

    Fresh Rajya Sabha notice, nine charges

    Jairam Ramesh (Congress) and Sagarika Ghose (TMC) submitted a new notice signed by 73 Rajya Sabha MPs, above the 50 required. It argued Kumar had issued no show-cause notice or public response to opposition complaints, in contrast to action on BJP complaints, and sought an inquiry committee and his recusal meanwhile.

    PTI via Daily ExcelsiorDT Next
  4. 27 May 2026Supreme Court

    Bihar SIR order upheld

    The Supreme Court upheld the ECI's 24 June 2025 order for the Bihar SIR, a ruling the Commission later cited in its defence on Form 6.

    PTI via The Federal
  5. 17 Jul 2026Allegation

    No word on the April notice

    Jairam Ramesh said the opposition had heard nothing on its April notice and would keep pressing for Kumar's removal.

    PTI via ThePrint
Phase 3

Dissent inside the Commission

September to October 2026. A newspaper investigation says the two Election Commissioners objected on record to decisions taken without them.

  1. 23 Sep 2026Media report

    Indian Express: 14 objections in 10 months

    The Indian Express reported that ECs Sukhbir Singh Sandhu and Vivek Joshi formally objected at least 14 times, on changes to Form 6, deletions and restorations, appeals, and centralised access to the ERONet roll database. It said the full Commission did not meet from May until 9 September, and that the two wrote to the Cabinet Secretary over a work-allocation order.

    ThePrintMoneylife
  2. 23 Sep 2026ECI · Press note

    "Decisions unanimous"

    The ECI said all its decisions, including SIR, carried the approval of all three members, and that commissioners' observations during drafting were routine in a multi-member body. It added that the commissioners had only made suggestions, which were examined. Reuters reported that Kumar and the two ECs did not comment personally.

    ThePrintUNIReuters via Kuwait Times
  3. 23 Sep 2026Allegation

    Opposition demands resignation

    Congress president Mallikarjun Kharge, Kapil Sibal and Shiv Sena (UBT) MP Sanjay Raut demanded Kumar's resignation, suspension or prosecution. Sibal said the report showed the majority of the Commission was not with the CEC.

    Telangana TodayPTI via ThePrint
  4. 24 Sep 2026Allegation

    Gandhi: resign and "turn approver"

    At a press conference Gandhi demanded Kumar's immediate resignation, framed the issue as "vote chori, kanoon chori, sanstha chori", and questioned the 2023 law's immunity clause and the appointment process. Gyanesh Kumar should think seriously and turn approver. The opposition began preparing a third removal notice in both Houses.

    UNIOutlookThe Week
  5. 26 Sep 2026ECI · Press note

    Full-Commission meeting and detailed note

    Kumar, Sandhu and Joshi met at Nirvachan Sadan. The ECI issued a press note with a photograph of all three, addressing new-voter enrolment and Form 6, the SIR schedule, ECINET access, and the letter the two ECs sent to the Cabinet Secretary. It said the Form 6 declaration had been upheld by the Supreme Court.

    Telangana TodayNewsBytes
  6. 5 Oct 2026Supreme Court

    No suspension for now; notice issued

    A bench of CJI Surya Kant and Justices Joymalya Bagchi and V. Mohana declined an ex-parte order suspending Kumar and issued notice to the Centre, the ECI, the CEC, both ECs and two senior officials. The petitions seek suspension, an SIT or judicial inquiry, and a ruling that decisions lacking the majority's concurrence are void.

    LiveLawUNI
  7. 5 Oct 2026ECI · Fact check

    Form 6 claim called "misleading"

    Responding to Gandhi's post that Kumar changed Form 6 illegally and unilaterally, the ECI said the Bihar SIR order of 24 June 2025 had unanimous approval and was upheld by the Supreme Court on 27 May 2026. It also denied withdrawing its September press note from its website.

    PTI via The Federal

Where things stand (10 Oct 2026)

  • The Supreme Court petitions are pending after notice; Kumar remains in office.
  • The 24 April Rajya Sabha removal notice was reported as still pending in late September. A third notice was being prepared; I could not confirm from reliable reports whether it has been filed.
  • The ECI's position is unchanged: its decisions were unanimous and lawful, and the allegations are baseless. Kumar has not given a fresh press conference on the dissent report.
  • Rahul Gandhi has not filed the declaration under oath that the ECI demanded in August 2025.

Maharashtra · Lok Sabha May 2024 → Vidhan Sabha Nov 2024 · Checked against official rolls

Did Maharashtra's voter rolls swell before the Assembly election?

This page checks the opposition's claim of an abnormal rise in registered voters between the Lok Sabha and Assembly elections of 2024. Lok Sabha electors come from the Election Commission of India (ECI). Assembly electors come from the Chief Electoral Officer (CEO), Maharashtra, final roll of 30 Oct 2024. Results come from ECI. All counts are general electors. Prepared 11 Oct 2026.

Verdict

Net new electors in ~5 months+40.81 lakh9,29,43,890 → 9,70,25,119 (+4.39%)
Lok Sabha seats above +10%1 of 48Kalyan, +10.26%
Seats where electors fell0 of 48Smallest rise: Gadchiroli–Chimur, +1.96%
Assembly seats won by Mahayuti230 of 288BJP 132, Shiv Sena 57, NCP 41

The statewide number is confirmed. Official figures show a net gain of 40.81 lakh electors between the two elections. By The Wire's compilation of ECI data, the 2019 gain for the same interval was 11.56 lakh.

Large rises were concentrated, not universal. Growth was highest in the urban belt of Thane, Palghar, Pune and Nagpur. Only Kalyan crossed +10% as a whole Lok Sabha seat, and no seat recorded a net fall.

Growth tracks results only weakly. Mahayuti won 76% of assembly seats inside the lowest-growth Lok Sabha seats and 83–90% inside the higher-growth ones. The correlation across the 48 seats is 0.17. That pattern is consistent with additions helping at the margin, but it does not prove the additions decided outcomes.

Lead = winner's votes minus runner-up's votes, as a % of votes polled, averaged over the assembly seats each side won in that band.

Mahayuti's average lead is largest in the highest-growth band (23.0%, against 15.6–18.8% where growth was under 4%). MVA's leads also rise with growth, from 4.8–8.7% to 17.3%, though MVA won only 3 seats in that band. Because leads widened for both alliances, this pattern alone does not show that the added voters caused the wider leads.

Mahayuti = BJP, Shiv Sena, NCP. MVA = INC, Shiv Sena (UBT), NCP (SP). The 12 other winners (small allies, independents, SP, AIMIM, CPI(M), PWPI) are counted in neither column.

How to read this. In 78 seats, more voters were added to the roll than the winner's margin of victory. That shows where additions were large enough to matter. It does not show the added voters decided these seats: they did not all vote, and they did not all vote for the winner.

The pattern cuts both ways. Mahayuti won 68% of these 78 close seats, less than its 80% share of all seats. MVA won 22 of them, including Karjat Jamkhed, Vadgaon Sheri, Beed, Jogeshwari East and Versova. Close seats were close for both sides.

Voters added per assembly seat is estimated from its Lok Sabha seat's growth rate, because official assembly-level Lok Sabha counts are not available. The five seats marked ✓ use actual RTI figures. Margin % = (winner − runner-up) ÷ total votes polled.

What this check cannot yet show

  • A full assembly-by-assembly list above 10% or below −10%. This needs Lok Sabha 2024 electors for each assembly segment. The ECI publishes them in its statistical reports and the CEO in Form 20, but both sites blocked automated access from here. If you upload that file (Excel or PDF), all 288 seats can be computed, including any that fell.
  • Exact additions per assembly seat. The margin comparison above uses estimates for 283 of 288 seats. With the official assembly-level Lok Sabha counts, the 78-seat list can be confirmed or corrected seat by seat.
  • Who was added. Net figures cannot separate genuine new voters (first-time 18–19 year olds, migrants) from duplicates. That needs roll-level audit data.
